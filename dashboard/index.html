How the value is computed

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth factor × age factor × chain factor

Category multiple

The median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the protocols in the same category whose token already trades, taken from this snapshot at run time. A category with fewer than three trading tokens uses the overall median. Multiples are capped at 1.00× TVL.

Growth factor

From the seven-day TVL change: 1 + half the change, clamped to the range 0.80–1.60. A protocol growing 40% in a week scores 1.20; one down 40% scores 0.80. No change reported scores 1.00.

Age factor

From the days since DefiLlama began tracking it: 1 + a quarter of the share of four years, so a four-year-old protocol with no token scores 1.25 and a new one scores near 1.00.

Chain factor

1 + 0.04 per extra chain, up to six chains. A protocol live on several chains reaches more users and more airdrop surfaces.

Multiples by category

A board row is an implied value, the entry’s own claim — never a prediction that a token will be issued, or when. A protocol “records no token” means its DefiLlama record carries none; it may still have a token the record does not carry.

Set aside: not a launch

Pre-token protocols the house watchlist rule leaves out, and why. Centralised exchanges, bridges and liquid-staking products are not protocols waiting on a launch of their own.
